# Non-Domiciliary: Bank Account 

<!--
On 5/28/2026, Surrogate Gingold presented to the TTT on when ancillary probate is required for a decedent who is not domiciled in New York. I learned that bank accounts (and accounts in other financial institutions) might require ancillary probate in New York. The general rule is that these assets are intangible and probate follows domicle. But there is an exception: when all branches of a bank account are in New York, ancillary probate in New York may be required. See: Otter recording and handouts that Surrogate Gingold gave.
